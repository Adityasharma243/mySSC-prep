Algebra

Notes

Most SSC algebra questions are solved by using an identity, not by finding x and y one by one. Look for squares, cubes and a sum or product that is given to you.

Formula sheet

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
(a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b)
(a + b)³ = a³ + b³ + 3ab(a + b)
a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² − ab + b²)
If x + 1/x = k, then x² + 1/x² = k² − 2
